    SMALL TEAM. PRACTICAL FIRST STEP.

    Built around the people who already care.

    Start with field updates. Add sensors where they help your team.

    A manageable pilot.

    • One colony, a care lead, and a backup.Agree on review ownership and check-ins before expanding.
    • Phone-friendly updates, with or without hardware.The sample batch and form work without a connected Arduino.
    • Keep uncertainty visible.Humans confirm identities, welfare follow-ups, and procedure evidence.
    • Measure whether it helps.Compare reconciliation minutes, duplicate records, and follow-up times against a manual baseline. No savings are claimed yet.

    Working local prototype built with Codex. No chat account is accessed and no message is sent. Hosted team access, multilingual support, and automated extraction are future pilot work.
